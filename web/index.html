VISTA JKLU - Advanced Hostel Management

VISTA (Virtual Intelligent Student Tracking & Attendance) is the official portal for JK Lakshmipat University students and staff.

  • AI Face Recognition Attendance: Secure and automated attendance tracking.
  • Automated Leave Requests: Easy submission and tracking of student leaves.
  • Short Stay Management: Hassle-free short stay applications for day scholars.
  • Complaint Portal: Direct communication with wardens and university staff.
  • Warden Dashboards: Comprehensive management tools for hostel administration.

Ensuring safety, discipline, and efficiency in university hostel operations.
